# Hyphaeic

**Topological compute and agency for ecological systems.**

Hyphaeic SPC is a research laboratory incorporated as a Social Purpose
Corporation in Washington State.

---

## The question

> How do purposeful systems made of partially informed, independently acting
> parts remain coherent, viable, and corrigible through change without complete
> representation, central control, or terminal optimisation?

We study this question from first principles in mathematics, computing, control,
embodiment, and governance.

## The problem

Every intelligent system acts through representations: a model, measurement,
reward, map, policy, price, or rule. Each representation leaves something out.

The structural failure begins when a system mistakes its representation for the
world, then applies increasing optimisation pressure to it. That failure appears
in reward-maximising AI, brittle central control, institutions governed by proxy
metrics, and growth that consumes the systems that make it possible.

We are not opposed to optimisation, modelling, control, markets, hierarchy, or
growth. We oppose treating any incomplete representation as sufficient to govern
the whole of what it represents.

## The approach

| Layer | Question | Work |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Representation** | How should locality, scale, time, and relationship be represented? | **FDRS** |
| **Coherence** | How can local computational actors form a distributed whole? | **HyphaFabric** |
| **Determinacy** | How can independent actors refer to reproducible physical state? | **Modulus** |
| **Action** | How can an agent reason over feasible futures without reducing intent to reward? | **STOK-CORE** |
| **Embodiment** | How can an agent act from bounded perception and corrigible belief? | **Abzu** |
| **Substrate** | How can computing be native to heterogeneous autonomous actors? | **HyphaeicOS** |

The mathematical foundation is machine-checked in Lean 4. The current substrate
runs on x86, ARM, and RISC-V without a host operating system, directly drives IO
controllers, and is designed for replicable behaviour across platforms.

## The positive thesis

Act through incomplete representations. Preserve boundaries between
independently capable systems. Couple action to evidence. Keep disagreement
visible. Permit local autonomy within higher-order invariants. Accumulate trust
through history. Commit decisively while retaining the capacity to adapt.

For us, viability is not survival at any cost or indefinite expansion. It is the
capacity for continued purposeful action while conditions change. The relevant
form of growth cultivates, rather than systematically consumes, the viability
and generative possibility of the systems with which it becomes entangled.

## The company as an experiment

The same principles apply to Hyphaeic itself. We use the Social Purpose
Corporation form because financial return is essential but not a complete
representation of institutional purpose.

Our foundational work is open source. We will not build surveillance
infrastructure, systems optimised for engagement or dependency, or technologies
that erase the boundaries required for independent agency.
