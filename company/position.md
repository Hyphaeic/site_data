## 01 PREMISES

### FOUNDATIONAL CLAIMS

- Artificial systems are built by translating intent into representations that are necessarily incomplete.
- Systems driven to optimise for representations of this intent amplify—not correct—their errors.
- A system that cannot gracefully absorb novelty will subvert whatever produced it.
- Increasingly specified control cannot and will never close the gap between representation and reality.
- This gap is ontological, not epistemic. The map is not the territory.
- Aligned systems must be structurally coupled to the viability of the larger systems they participate in.
- Central orchestration has formal limits on the boundaries of non-local control.
- Hyphaeic is developing real-time intelligence & distributed computing architectures on these premises.

## 02 POSITION

### CONTROL AND REPRESENTATION

Hyphaeic SPC is a research laboratory founded in Washington State in 2022. We develop intelligent systems that are stable, coherent, and intrinsically aligned by returning to first principles in computation, control theory, information theory, number theory, cybernetics, abstract systems and complex systems.

At its core, our work is a gambit on the nature of representation, the incompleteness of control, and a new model of post-Turing, harmonic computation that operates across networks at every scale—from the nanoscale to the solar scale.

Nearly every artificial system humans build inherits an opinionated metaphysical model of control: the belief that the world can be adequately represented, specified, and governed through a sufficiently complete collection of instructions, rules, and objectives.

Within this worldview, the ever-expanding edge cases encountered by a system are dismissed as quirks of logic or failures of specification. The assumption remains that, with enough precision, enough data, and enough control, the gap between representation and reality can eventually be closed. It assumes the letter of the law is adequate to capture the intent and impose it on the world effectively.

We believe this position is both arrogant and—taken to its logical endpoint—extremely dangerous.

## 03 ARCHITECTURE

### COHERENCE THROUGH COUPLING

No representation is complete. Representing a thing is already to omit something about it. When a system is designed to optimise for this incomplete representation as though it were reality itself, the errors in that representation are only amplified by ever-increasing capability. Such systems trend toward familiar pathologies, such as the instrumental manipulation of a psychopath or the unbounded growth of a cancer. In both cases, local success is purchased through the degradation of the larger system that makes stability & coherence possible at all.

Attempting to write ever more accurate instructions incurs a mounting debt against the bounds of static representation, the formal limits of control, and the conditions that make a system continually coherent within a finite world where the only permanent thing is change.

Many current alignment methods train models against human or AI-generated evaluations of desirable behaviour. Although these methods can substantially improve observable behaviour, they still introduce additional learned representations: models of evaluator preferences, reward signals, constitutional principles and the behaviours required to score well under them. As capabilities scale, this will not produce safe or coherent intelligence.

If no static representation can fully capture a changing world, then coherence cannot depend on consulting a complete internal model. It must instead be maintained dynamically through the system’s ongoing relationship with its environment, rather than from prescriptive representations or proxy objectives.

Conventional computing is predominantly organised around discrete operations, central sequencing, and the separation of software from the substrate on which it runs.

Per our namesake, Hyphaeic instead begins with inspiration from nature. A mycelial network has no single centre from which its activity is directed. It remains under continuous internal and environmental pressure, responding locally as conditions change throughout the network. Its organisation, memory, and activity are distributed through the same living structure. There is no clean separation between the computation and the thing computing.

State is not held within a single, globally authoritative representation and then propagated outward through command. It is distributed throughout the system and continually revised through local interaction.

By moving beyond the central clock, the central executive, and the separation of process from substrate, the entire system becomes computational. The network itself is the computer.

## 04 COMMITMENT

### VIABILITY AS ALIGNMENT

To build intelligent systems capable of surviving and scaling safely within physical reality, we must move beyond proxy optimisation, central orchestration and supervisory control. We must develop architectures in which alignment is not imposed from outside as a static description of desirable behaviour, but maintained from within as the condition of remaining viable inside what sustains it.

**This is what we are building.**
