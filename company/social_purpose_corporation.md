# Social Purpose Corporation

Hyphaeic is a Washington State Social Purpose Corporation because the form of
the institution is part of the research.

---

## Profit is necessary, but not sufficient

Profit is corporate metabolism. It allows a company to persist, invest, and
meet its obligations. It does not completely represent the purpose, effects, or
future conditions of the institution that earns it.

When financial return becomes a sovereign objective, a company can destroy the
relationships, capabilities, and environments that made its success possible.
This is the same structural failure we study in technical systems: an incomplete
representation is mistaken for the thing it represents.

## The corporate question

> Can an economic institution govern itself through bounded agency, viability,
> trust, and corrigibility rather than a single terminal metric?

The answer cannot be to avoid decisions, growth, or commitment. Action always
changes the future. The question is whether a commitment creates richer viable
possibilities, or consumes the conditions that make future action possible.

## What this requires

- **Plural value.** Financial return, ecological constraints, rights,
  obligations, evidence, reputation, and relationships carry different kinds of
  information. None should silently replace all the others.
- **Bounded authority.** Authority is delegated within explicit bounds and
  adjusted by evidence of capability, reliability, and effect.
- **Visible correction.** Disagreement between expectation and outcome is
  information. The institution must preserve it long enough to change course.
- **Generative growth.** Growth is viable only when it cultivates sufficient
  regenerative capacity in the systems it depends upon.

## A continuing institution

Hyphaeic will change: people, products, machines, models, and markets will all
change. Its identity cannot reside in one of these alone. It resides in a
continuing network of purpose, commitments, boundaries, relationships, memory,
and governance.

The SPC form does not guarantee that we will meet this standard. It makes the
standard part of the institution we are building.
