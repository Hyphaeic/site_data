# Metaphysics

> STATUS: POSITION

The programme begins before distributed computing, robotics, or alignment.
It begins with a question about purposeful action under irreducible
incompleteness.

> How can a system act, adapt, and remain coherent when its representation of
> reality is incomplete, its future cannot be predicted perfectly, and neither
> it nor any part possesses the whole?

## What we reject

We do not reject optimisation, measurement, modelling, markets, hierarchy,
growth, or control. Each can be useful.

We reject the stronger assumptions that optimisation of a representation is
sufficient to preserve what it represents; that more central representation
necessarily creates coherence; that price or profit represents all value; that
hierarchy should erase local competence; and that sufficiently complete control
can remove uncertainty.

## Positive thesis

Act through incomplete representations. Preserve boundaries between
independently capable systems. Couple action to evidence. Keep disagreement
visible. Permit local autonomy inside higher-order invariants. Accumulate trust
through history. Commit decisively while retaining the ability to adapt.

The question beneath alignment follows directly: what does it mean for a
purposeful system to remain aligned with something that changes while every
representation of it remains partial? A complete answer requires perception,
correction, boundaries, history, embodiment, plural value, and continuity of
purpose.
