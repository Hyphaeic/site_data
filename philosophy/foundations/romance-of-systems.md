# A Romance of Systems

> STATUS: HYPOTHESIS

Trust is justified confidence in another actor's behaviour across uncertain
future states. It matters because uncertain futures make complete contracts
impossible.

## Trust and relationship

Accumulated history can allow increasingly complex cooperation under decreasing
completeness of specification. Trust is not niceness. It makes new joint
reachable states possible.

## Generative growth

Growth can create extraordinary capability, but growth that consumes its own
enabling conditions is not viable expansion. The question is not whether a
system has impact. It is whether its relationships create enough regenerative
capacity to continue.

This applies to companies and employees, firms and suppliers, economies and
their ecosystems, and AI platforms and their users.

## Institutional identity and governance

A changing system persists through more than any leader, model, shareholder,
or component. Institutional identity may reside in a continuing network of
purpose, commitments, boundaries, relationships, memory, governance, and
transformation.

Governance is therefore neither central command nor complete decentralisation.
It asks what must remain invariant at the higher level and what should stay
adaptive locally. Evidence moves upward; authority is delegated downward;
cross-boundary effects propagate to the level able to address them.
