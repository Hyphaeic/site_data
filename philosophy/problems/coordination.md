# Coordination

> STATUS: HYPOTHESIS

How do many local parts compose into one persistent whole without a central referee?

## The problem

A rack, a body, or a swarm contains actors with different state, histories,
rates, and failures. No actor contains the whole. Yet the larger system must
remain coherent enough to act.

## Failure mode

Central coordination creates a point of failure. Purely independent actors
diverge. Partitions can produce conflicting plans or deadlock.

## Direction

Coherence is engineered through local representation, bounded coupling, and
harmonic propagation. Islands can continue under partition and re-establish
relationships without a global state or permanent leader.
