# Embodiment

> STATUS: OPEN PROBLEM

A system with a body is of the world, not merely hosted in it; the boundary that ends it is conceptually important.

## The problem, physically
Embodiment forces locality: the system ends at the boundary of its internal network and control structure.

## The problem, formally
Freedom/optionality is the precondition of the property that makes a system safe, staged in practice.
