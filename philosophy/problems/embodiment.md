# Embodiment

> STATUS: OPEN PROBLEM

A system with a body is in the world, not merely hosted in it.

## The problem

How do local sensors, actuators, reflexes, and control loops produce coherent
action through latency, noise, partial failure, and real physics?

## Failure mode

Centralised optimisation assumes global state and tight synchronisation. Pure
learning systems can lose predictable behaviour under distribution shift or
hardware failure. Neither gives a general account of how local competence
becomes whole-body competence.

## Direction

Embodied control requires local models and local clocks, with coherence
propagated across control islands. The body participates in the computation;
the whole need not be continuously represented by a central planner.
