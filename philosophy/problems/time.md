# Time

> STATUS: OPEN PROBLEM

Different clocks at different resolutions; a system must stay one system across all of them.

## The problem, physically
A fingertip, a vision loop, and a planner do not share a clock. What does coherence mean across incommensurable timebases?

## The problem, formally
A logical clock decouples time from hardware speed, so order and bounds are provable rather than accidental.
