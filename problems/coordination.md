# Coordination

> STATUS: HYPOTHESIS

How do many local parts compose into one persistent whole without a central referee?

## The problem, physically
A rack, a body, a swarm: each part acts on local state. Somewhere a boundary must decide what is inside and what is outside, and that boundary is itself an actor.

## The problem, formally
We treat coherence not as an emergent nicety but as a constraint to be engineered: bounded gossip, local contracts, and boundaries that are themselves load-bearing.
