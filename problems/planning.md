# Planning

> STATUS: OPEN PROBLEM

How does a system plan by what keeps it viable rather than by maximizing a proxy?

## The problem, physically
Proxy-maximizing collapses the thing the proxy stood for. Planning must be over optionality and viability, not terminal reward.

## The problem, formally
State-Time Option Kernels generate feasibility maps over what-can-be-reached-from-here; viability is the objective.
