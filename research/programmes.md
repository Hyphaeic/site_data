# Programmes

> STATUS: POSITION

Hyphaeic studies one problem from several directions: how partially informed,
independently acting parts can remain coherent, viable, and corrigible without
complete representation, central control, or a terminal objective.

---

## FDRS: representation, scale, and time

**Question:** What remains valid when the counting base is itself a function of
context?

Function-Defined Radix Systems make radix a part of the state rather than a
fixed convention. The programme studies the resulting mixed-radix geometry,
its ultrametric locality, and the structure exposed when addition and
multiplication do not commute.

## Harmonic multiclock computation

**Question:** How can components operating at different rates remain coherent
without sharing a master clock?

The work treats time as local logical clocks with known relationships, not as a
single global tick. Harmonic islands may split, continue locally, and
re-establish coherence when communication returns.

## Local-to-global coherence

**Question:** What relationships between local actors are sufficient to
produce a persistent whole?

This programme investigates local representation, deterministic propagation,
bounded coupling, partitions, and re-coherence. It applies to a machine, a
network, a body, and a collective without assuming that any part contains the
whole.

## State-time optionality

**Question:** How can an agent choose among feasible futures without reducing
purpose to reward maximisation?

State-Time Option Kernels represent reachable states and times as compositional
kernels. The programme studies planning by viability, optionality, and
commitment, including the residual between declared and realised time.

## Embedded intent and corrigibility

**Question:** How can a system act from an incomplete model while retaining the
ability to discover that its model is wrong?

The work treats disagreement between expectation and outcome as a durable
signal. It asks how evidence, consequence, and local revision can maintain
continuity of purpose under novelty.

## Embodied intelligence

**Question:** How do local sensor, actuator, and control loops constitute
coherent action in real physics?

The programme connects multiclock computation and option kernels to bodies
whose components have different latencies, histories, and failure modes.

## Collective intelligence and governance

**Question:** How can independently capable actors cooperate without erasing
their boundaries or local competence?

This work covers trust, authority, boundaries, institutional identity, and
polycentric governance. It asks how authority can be delegated according to
evidence and withdrawn when residuals or boundary violations accumulate.

## Generative viability

**Question:** What must a system preserve in order to remain capable of
purposeful action in a changing world?

Viability is not stasis or indefinite self-preservation. The programme studies
commitments that create richer future possibilities, and growth that cultivates
rather than consumes the systems on which it depends.
